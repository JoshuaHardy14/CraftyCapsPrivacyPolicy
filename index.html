Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

In short: our games have no accounts, no sign-up and no chat. We do not collect your name, email or location. Your progress stays on your device. The only data that leaves it is what Google handles to show you optional ads and to process a purchase, as described below.

1. Who we are

CraftyCaps (“we”, “us”) is an independent games studio. This policy covers every game we publish, including the games listed in section 10. If you have a question about it, write to us at craftycaps364@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

Our games store your progress, settings, character looks, achievements, statistics and a saved run in files on your device. We never receive this data. You can erase it at any time from the game’s Settings (Data, Clear Save Data), or by clearing the game’s storage or uninstalling it in your phone’s settings.

3. Data we collect

We do not run our own servers for our games and we do not ask you to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information such as your name, address, email address, phone number or precise location.

4. Advertising

Our games may offer you the choice to watch a rewarded video ad in return for a reward in the game, for example a free continue after you are defeated. An ad is only shown when you tap a button that says so. We do not show banner or pop-up ads, and nothing plays by itself.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating-system details, and how you interact with ads. This happens under Google’s own terms and not ours; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Your consent choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland), the first time you start the game it shows a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asking how ads may use your data. You can change your choice later in the game under Settings, Data, Privacy Options (the button appears when your region allows a change).

You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

6. Purchases

The games may offer an optional one-time purchase, such as removing ads and unlocking everything. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not see or store your payment details. We only receive confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made, and the game keeps a small record of it on your device so that it can unlock the content. The game also asks Google Play what you own when it starts, so that your purchase returns on a new phone or after a reinstall.

The games use Unity’s in-app purchasing software to talk to Google Play. It may process purchase and device information as described in Unity’s privacy notice.

7. Children

Our games are not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Parents and guardians of younger players can control ad personalisation in the device settings described above. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR and EU GDPR) you may have rights over the data that Google or other third parties hold about you, and you can exercise them with those companies. You may contact us with any request and we will answer within a reasonable time.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when a new game or feature changes how data is used. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

10. Our games

  • MobFighter: a third-person action game for Android. It uses rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and one optional purchase (Google Play), as described above.
